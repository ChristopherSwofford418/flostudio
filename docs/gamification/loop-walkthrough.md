# Flo Momentum Loop Walkthrough

1. **Truth.** The operator selects a portfolio app and confirms or saves real product facts and Brand DNA. The Product Truth stage remains incomplete until there is a persisted product/brand record with meaningful product context.

2. **Thesis.** The operator creates campaign concepts, compares two when useful, edits hook, proof, CTA, or visual direction, and explicitly selects a thesis. The Runbook records why this is meaningful: the campaign now has an inspectable strategic choice rather than an opaque generation request.

3. **Creative family.** The operator attaches two completed, campaign-linked assets or outputs. Each member has a visible lineage and a concise change summary. A queued, failed, or unlinked render remains a draft.

4. **Human review.** The operator or an authorized reviewer saves an approval, revision, rejection, or hold decision. Approval does not publish anything; the decision simply makes the review record inspectable.

5. **Controlled experiment.** The operator creates a hypothesis, primary metric, control, and one challenger with a single-variable change summary. Flo labels missing conditions as blockers rather than inferring readiness.

6. **Verified learning.** The operator records a numeric observation with its unit, source, timestamp, and decision. A Learning Statement describes what changed, what the evidence showed, and what to reuse or avoid. Inconclusive is a legitimate decision when the evidence is insufficient.

7. **Reuse.** The operator may explicitly promote a linked learning to Creative Memory. Flo can offer it as a transparent, editable draft for the next campaign; it never automatically overwrites Brand DNA or claims that the creative will win again.

## Pause and archive

At any point, the operator can pause a runbook without explanation, archive it with optional context, and reopen it later. A runbook remains part of the user’s portfolio record regardless of campaign status, token balance, or subscription state.
