# Flo Momentum Ethical Risk Register

| Risk | Preventive control | Detection / rollback signal |
|---|---|---|
| Fake progress from clicks, page loads, tokens, or generated drafts | `campaignMomentum` evaluates only persisted records with linked campaign/product lineage. Tokens, routes, and local storage are never inputs. | Any checkpoint without inspectable evidence is shown as unavailable or incomplete and the display should be disabled. |
| False performance claim | Outcome completion requires a numeric observation, unit, source, observed-at timestamp, human decision, and learning statement. | A missing observation or reflection must surface “needs evidence,” never winner language. |
| Publication pressure | Approval records are distinct from publishing; no runbook action calls social publishing. | Review action unexpectedly sends a provider request or changes published status. Remove the integration immediately. |
| Manipulative urgency | No daily cadence, countdown, streak, or expiring work is modeled. | Copy that frames pause, archive, or an inconclusive outcome as lost momentum must be revised. |
| Cross-product leakage | RLS and service queries scope every runbook record to workspace, user, and product. | A product’s Learning Review can show another product’s content without its source metadata or owner-approved draft flow. |
| Claim and compliance risk | Product truth and claim restrictions remain visible before thesis, family, and experiment work. | A concept, adaptation, or learning summary introduces unsupported product or outcome language. |
| Irreversible data loss | Runbooks use pause/archive controls and retain evidence; deletion is separate and explicit. | Routine state transitions delete a runbook, experiment, reflection, or linked asset. |
| Monetization confusion | Generation costs are quoted by existing workflows and never advance Momentum. | Tokens or tier are presented as achievement, progress, or a requirement for review/learning. |
| Review ambiguity | Review decisions record reviewer, timestamp, status, and optional reason in immutable rows. | A campaign is called reviewed based solely on a post draft or a mutable status without a decision record. |
| Recommendation opacity | Learning reuse summary names source product, campaign, experiment, and evidence limitations. | A recommended thesis is presented as predicted performance or lacks a traceable source. |

## Operator protections

Flo Momentum is not a behavioral incentive program. Operators can keep exploring, pause a campaign, record that a direction was not a fit, save an inconclusive observation, archive a runbook, and return later. These actions retain their work and do not reduce access to it.
