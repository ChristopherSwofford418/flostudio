# Flo Momentum Technique Map

| Core drive / technique | Flo Studio implementation | Durable state | User control | Evidence and removal condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CD1 — Credible purpose | “Why Momentum exists” explains that useful campaign evidence matters more than output volume. | Campaign Runbook and product/campaign records. | Dismiss help and continue with any workflow. | Remove wording if it implies mission, identity, or impact that Flo cannot substantiate. |
| CD2 — Evidence-backed milestones | Seven inspectable Campaign Runbook stages with real completion reasons. | Checkpoints are derived from persisted campaign, review, experiment, and reflection records; overrides only pause or archive a runbook. | Open evidence, pause, archive, reopen, hide progress. | Remove a milestone if no linked durable artifact can be opened. |
| CD2 — Controlled experiment | A “Boss Fight” label is optional explanatory language for a testable hypothesis with a control and one challenger. | Experiment, variants, observations, and outcome records. | Edit, pause, archive, or record an inconclusive result. | Never use a timer or describe an unsourced outcome as a win. |
| CD3 — Thesis comparison | Compare two editable theses by hook, proof, CTA, visual direction, claim notes, and platform fit. | Campaign concepts and selected concept pointer. | Keep exploring, select, save as reference, dismiss. | Remove comparison claims that do not reflect stored concept fields. |
| CD3 — Controlled variation | Creative Family groups completed, campaign-linked assets and shows the changed variable. | Media asset lineage, campaign links, and variant change summaries. | Attach an existing asset, request revision, approve, reject, hold. | Do not show a queued, failed, or detached render as a completed family member. |
| CD3 — Free boosters | “Switch proof,” “change opening visual,” and “narrow audience” create editable starting drafts only. | Existing editable concept fields. | Ignore, edit, or choose another route. | No paid escape, hidden AI result, or automatic change. |
| CD4 — Campaign Runbook | Editable and exportable record of campaign facts, decisions, evidence, and learning. | `campaign_runbooks`, reflections, review decisions, and linked evidence. | Archive, reopen, delete, export, and edit statements. | Never hide an existing runbook because of a tier or token balance. |
| CD4 — Creative Memory reuse | Transparent source-linked suggestion from verified learning. | Creative Memory event plus linked experiment/reflection. | Use as draft, edit, not now, or do not suggest again. | Do not offer cross-product reuse without origin and reason. |
| CD5 — Private review only | Review attribution remains a consented workspace workflow. | Authenticated reviewer and immutable review decision. | Review is optional; social sharing is not introduced. | Defer collaboration extensions until role and audit controls are present. |

## Restricted mechanisms

CD6 through CD8 are excluded unless Flo can show a factual, user-benefiting constraint and an opt-out. The implementation contains no streaks, countdowns, artificial deadlines, scarcity, random rewards, penalty messaging, or payment-driven relief.
