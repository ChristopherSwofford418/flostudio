Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 30, 2026

FloStudio has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We prohibit any content or activity that exploits, endangers, sexualizes, grooms, traffics, or otherwise harms a child.

Prohibited content and conduct

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including real, illustrated, synthetic, or AI-generated material.
  • Sexualization of minors, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or solicitation of sexual contact with a minor.
  • Requests, instructions, promotion, normalization, or facilitation of child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Sharing links, files, accounts, or instructions intended to obtain or distribute prohibited material.

Enforcement

We may remove prohibited material, restrict or terminate accounts, preserve relevant records, and report apparent CSAM or child exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and appropriate law-enforcement authorities when required or appropriate.

How to report a concern

Use the Report a Safety Concern option in FloStudio, or email our designated child-safety contact at info@clearpasstechnologies.com. Include enough information to locate the relevant content or account. Do not attach or redistribute suspected CSAM.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Reports involving apparent online child sexual exploitation may also be made to NCMEC’s CyberTipline.

Review and cooperation

Child-safety reports are prioritized for review. FloStudio cooperates with lawful investigations and maintains processes intended to prevent known violators from continuing prohibited activity.
