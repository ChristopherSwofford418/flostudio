# Competitor Platform Notes — August 2026

## Creatify

- **Workflow breadth:** Creatify positions its system around discovery, creation, and launch: ad-library/competitor research, URL-to-video and ad-flow creation, batch variations, connected ad accounts, and A/B testing. Source: https://creatify.ai/
- **Creative modalities:** Its published feature set includes URL-to-video, product video, image-to-video, AI avatars and custom avatars, AI influencers/humans, text-to-speech, hooks/scripts, captions, b-roll/music, automated editing, ad cloning, and multi-format output. Source: https://creatify.ai/features
- **Scale and repeatability:** Creatify documents batch generation and reusable/custom templates. Its API covers URL-to-video, avatar/video, custom templates, asset/image generation, ad cloning, IAB image ads, and inspiration templates. Sources: https://creatify.ai/features and https://docs.creatify.ai/introduction
- **Commercial operating model:** Its API is a distinct product surface, enabling programmatic video creation and templates for customers who require scale. Source: https://creatify.ai/api

## Holo

- **Product wedge:** Holo centers the product on URL onboarding, persistent Brand DNA, daily idea delivery, lightweight editing, and publishing across social/email rather than maximum video-production depth. Source: https://tryholo.ai/
- **Brand intelligence:** Holo claims to infer brand style, audience pain points, and purchase triggers, then use that context across its content engine. Source: https://tryholo.ai/

## AdCreative.ai

- **Performance wedge:** AdCreative.ai emphasizes website ingestion, brand import, format/size variation, creative scoring, competitor insight, buyer personas, creative fatigue analysis, product photoshoots, image/video generation, and A/B-testing orientation. Source: https://www.adcreative.ai/

## FloStudio Current Baseline

- FloStudio has campaign post creation and persistence, a content calendar, review states, real asset records, GPT-image generation, an asynchronous video-provider route, Meta OAuth initiation, token gating, a cross-workspace media library, and media-first review-card generation.
- It lacks the coherent end-to-end inputs and control layers that make the leaders feel complete: URL ingestion and Brand DNA, multi-scene ad composition/editing, personas/avatars/voices, creative research and scoring grounded in evidence, bulk generation with experiment tracking, production template systems, and actual paid-media launch/measurement loops.
