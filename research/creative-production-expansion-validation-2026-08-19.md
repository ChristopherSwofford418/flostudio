The production test selected the newest saved image from the Asset Library and used its Review action. The library confirmed: **“Creative is now in the Review Queue as a pending Instagram draft.”** No social platform was published to; the action created an internal review record only.

The production Review Queue was then opened and displayed the new pending card with the saved AI image attached, its selected creative label, and the operator’s hook and proof copy. This confirms the full internal path: **real render → tenant media library → pending campaign draft → review decision surface**.
