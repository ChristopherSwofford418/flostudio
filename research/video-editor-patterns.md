# Video Editor Workflow Research

## CapCut workflow patterns relevant to Creative Lab

CapCut documents an AI video workflow that moves from a script and visual style into scenes, voiceover, music, subtitles, a timeline/editor, and export settings. Its script-to-video guidance also distinguishes automated generation from using a creator's own local media.

FloStudio should adapt this for app marketing by retaining a selected app image as product truth, using it for readable app moments, and placing the UGC creator, story shape, hook, proof, and CTA controls in one focused production workspace.
